## Introduction to Unacademy
Unacademy is one of the most popular online learning platforms in India, offering a wide range of courses and specializations to help students achieve their career goals. Founded in 2015 by Gaurav Munjal, Roman Saini, and Hemesh Singh, Unacademy has come a long way in providing high-quality educational content to millions of students across the country.

### What is Unacademy?
Unacademy is an online learning platform that provides courses and specializations in various fields, including science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM), humanities, and social sciences. The platform offers live classes, video lectures, and interactive quizzes to help students learn and engage with the course material.

### History and Milestones
Unacademy was founded with the mission of providing accessible and affordable education to students in India. Over the years, the platform has achieved several milestones, including:
* Partnering with top Indian universities, such as the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) and the Indian Institute of Management (IIM)
* Reaching over 10 million registered students
* Offering over 1,000 courses and specializations
* Expanding its services to include test preparation, career counseling, and placement assistance

Unacademy has also received funding from top investors, including Sequoia Capital, SAIF Partners, and Blume Ventures, to name a few. With its strong backing and commitment to providing high-quality education, Unacademy has become one of the most trusted online learning platforms in India. You can visit their website to learn more about their offerings: [Unacademy Website](https://unacademy.com)

Unacademy offers a wide range of courses and specializations to cater to the diverse needs of students in India. The platform provides courses in various fields, including:

### Course Catalog

* Test preparation: IIT JEE, NEET, CAT, and other competitive exams

## Pricing and Plans
Unacademy offers various pricing plans to suit the needs of different students. The platform provides both individual plans and subscription models, allowing students to choose the option that best fits their budget and learning goals.

### Individual Plans
Unacademy's individual plans are designed for students who want to take a specific course or specialization. The cost of individual plans varies depending on the course, but most courses are priced between ₹5,000 to ₹50,000.

### Subscription Models
Unacademy's subscription models provide students with access to a wide range of courses and specializations for a fixed monthly or yearly fee. The platform offers two subscription models:
* **Unacademy Plus**: ₹1,500 per month or ₹15,000 per year
* **Unacademy Pro**: ₹3,000 per month or ₹30,000 per year

The Unacademy Plus subscription model provides students with access to over 1,000 courses and specializations, while the Unacademy Pro subscription model offers additional benefits, including:
* Personalized career counseling
* Placement assistance
* Access to exclusive events and webinars
