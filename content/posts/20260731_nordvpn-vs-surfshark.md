## Introduction to NordVPN and Surfshark
India has seen a significant rise in VPN usage over the past few years, and two of the most popular VPNs among Indian users are NordVPN and Surfshark. Both VPNs have been around for a while and have gained a reputation for providing top-notch security and features.

### Overview of NordVPN
NordVPN is one of the oldest and most well-established VPNs in the market. Founded in 2012, NordVPN has its headquarters in Panama and has become a household name in the VPN industry. With over 5,000 servers in 60 countries, NordVPN offers a wide range of features, including strong encryption, a no-logs policy, and support for up to 6 devices.

### Overview of Surfshark
Surfshark, on the other hand, is a relatively new player in the VPN market. Founded in 2018, Surfshark has quickly gained popularity among Indian users due to its affordable pricing and robust features. With over 3,200 servers in 65 countries, Surfshark offers a similar set of features to NordVPN, including strong encryption, a no-logs policy, and support for unlimited devices.

Both NordVPN and Surfshark have gained a significant following in India due to their ability to bypass geo-restrictions and provide access to popular streaming services like Netflix, Amazon Prime, and Hotstar.
