## Introduction to Unacademy
Unacademy is one of the most popular online learning platforms in India, offering a wide range of courses for competitive exams, professional development, and skill-based learning. Founded in 2015 by Gaurav Munjal, Roman Saini, and Hemesh Singh, Unacademy has become a household name in India, with over 10 million registered users as of 2026.

### What is Unacademy?
Unacademy is an online education platform that provides video lectures, live classes, and study materials for various exams and courses. It covers a broad range of subjects, including UPSC, SSC, Banking, Engineering, and more. The platform uses a unique approach, where top educators and experts create engaging video content to help students learn and prepare for their exams.

### History and Founders
Unacademy was founded by three young entrepreneurs who were passionate about education and technology. Gaurav Munjal, the CEO, has a strong background in education and technology, while Roman Saini, the Chief Educator, is a renowned educator and expert in the field of UPSC preparation. Hemesh Singh, the CTO, is a skilled technologist who has developed the platform's infrastructure. Together, they have built Unacademy into a leading online learning platform in India.

## Unacademy Courses and Features
Unacademy offers a vast array of courses, catering to the needs of students preparing for competitive exams, professionals looking to upskill, and individuals seeking to learn new skills.

### Course Catalog
Unacademy's course catalog includes:
* Competitive exams: UPSC, SSC, Banking, Engineering, and more
* Professional courses: Data Science, Machine Learning, Digital Marketing, and more
* Skill-based courses: Programming, Photography, Graphic Design, and more

### Live Classes and Doubt Sessions
Unacademy's live classes and doubt sessions are highly interactive, allowing students to engage with educators and clear their doubts in real-time. These sessions are conducted by top educators and experts in their respective fields.

### Mobile App
Unacademy's mobile app is available for both Android and iOS devices, providing students with access to their courses and study materials on-the-go. The app is user-friendly, with features like video playback, notes, and reminders.
